# Direction: Broad / universal

## Audience

Mixed-age local visitors (szeroka grupa odbiorców) who need Auto Szkoła Juszczak’s offer to feel friendly and obvious — not fashion-led, youth-coded, or corporate-luxury.

## Mood

Friendly, clear, universal — calm hierarchy and neighborhood approachability; welcoming without being cute or loud.

## Palette keywords

Daylight white and soft cool gray bases; warm charcoal text; gentle sky-blue or soft teal accent for links/CTAs; mid-contrast supports; accents stay supportive, never electric or metallic-luxury.

## Typography vibe

Approachable rounded-or-open sans for brand and UI; clear mid-weight headlines; generous body size and line-height for mixed-age reading; avoid condensed display energy and stiff luxury serifs.

## Layout/UI cues

Simple section stacking with one job per block; predictable nav and contact paths; hero stays full-bleed but with calmer typography and less kinetic motion; large touch targets; icon use only when it aids scanning; prefer open layouts over card grids unless interaction needs a container.

## Pros

- Lowest risk of alienating parents, older candidates, or mixed households browsing the same phone.
- Clarity-first hierarchy helps the existing offer facts land without redesigning copy or inventing claims.
- Friendly accent + calm neutrals read as “local school you can call,” not a campaign microsite.
- Easiest baseline for later polish — energy or formality can be tuned up after lock if feedback asks.

## Anti-goals

- Do not chase youth energy (sharp neon accents, aggressive motion) or business luxury (sparse prestige minimalism).
- Avoid cluttered pill clusters, stat strips, and multi-promo first viewports that fight a clear primary path.
