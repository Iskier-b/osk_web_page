# Direction: Young drivers

## Audience

Younger and first-time course candidates (młodzi kierowcy) comparing local OSKs on a phone — people who expect Auto Szkoła Juszczak to feel current, fast, and worth a tap.

## Mood

Dynamic, modern, energetic — motion-forward confidence without looking like a gaming or nightlife brand.

## Palette keywords

Cool graphite neutrals; crisp off-white surfaces; one high-contrast signal accent (electric lime or sharp cyan); deep ink for body text; spare use of accent for CTAs and focus states only.

## Typography vibe

Tight, geometric sans for brand and headlines; slightly condensed display weight for hero impact; clean readable sans for body; bold size jumps and short lines over soft editorial serifs.

## Layout/UI cues

Full-bleed hero as the main visual plane; strong vertical rhythm and asymmetric blocks; generous CTA scale; intentional scroll motion (2–3 purposeful reveals); mobile-first density with clear tap targets; avoid card grids in the hero and pill-stat clutter.

## Pros

- Matches how younger candidates scan competitor schools on mobile — high energy, clear primary action.
- Accent-led hierarchy makes “zapisz się / kontakt” impossible to miss without adding extra promo chrome.
- Distinct from corporate brochure OSK sites, so the demo stands out in a side-by-side phone comparison.
- Motion budget supports presence without needing dense content blocks in the first viewport.

## Anti-goals

- No neon glow stacks, purple-to-indigo gradients, or sticker/badge overlays on hero media.
- Do not lean so “youth app” that trust cues (instructor, offer, contact) feel secondary or unserious.
