# Direction: Business clients

## Audience

Candidates and surrounding decision-makers (klienci biznesowi) who treat the course as a serious service purchase and judge Auto Szkoła Juszczak on polish, clarity, and credibility.

## Mood

Elegant, minimal, professional — calm authority and restraint; quiet confidence rather than campaign energy.

## Palette keywords

Charcoal and soft stone neutrals; paper-white fields; restrained steel-blue or slate accent used sparingly; low-saturation surfaces; no loud complementary pops.

## Typography vibe

Refined humanist or neo-grotesque sans with generous tracking in the brand lockup; measured headline scale; comfortable long-line body; hierarchy through weight and spacing, not decorative display fonts.

## Layout/UI cues

Wide margins and disciplined whitespace; single-column clarity on key sections; understated separators instead of card chrome; CTA as a precise primary button, not a loud promo band; photography as atmosphere with restrained crop and no floating labels; motion limited to subtle fades/settles.

## Pros

- Signals a trustworthy service purchase for adults comparing schools as a practical decision, not a trend.
- Minimal chrome keeps offer facts and contact paths readable without youth-marketing noise.
- Restraint photographs well in demos to stakeholders who expect “professional site,” not startup flash.
- Easy to keep brand “Auto Szkoła Juszczak” as the quiet hero signal without competing decorative type.

## Anti-goals

- No luxury-hotel gold/cream theatrics, terracotta-and-serif lifestyle identity, or dense brochure layouts.
- Avoid dark-mode default and multi-layer shadows that make a local OSK feel like a SaaS product.
