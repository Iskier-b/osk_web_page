# Audience & business brief

## Business character

Auto Szkoła Juszczak is a local driving school (OSK) whose current public site is https://www.autojuszczak.com.pl/. The redesign demo is a proof of concept / market-feedback piece for the OSK owner: show a modern presentation of the **existing** offer so more course candidates can imagine enrolling — not invent new copywriting, products, or claims. Structure and source-faithful facts stay; look and UX are redesigned.

This brief does not recommend which visual direction to lock. It only explains why page_mvp’s three example audiences are the right comparison set for this demo.

## Audiences (from page_mvp §5)

Labels and mood cues below match page_mvp’s examples exactly (Polish labels in parentheses). No fourth competing frame.

### Young drivers — dynamic / modern / energetic (młodzi kierowcy)

First-time and younger course candidates are a core inflow for a local OSK; a dynamic, modern, energetic direction makes the demo feel current and worth opening on a phone when they are comparing schools.

### Business clients — elegant / minimal / professional (klienci biznesowi)

Some candidates (and decision-makers around them) evaluate the school as a serious service purchase; an elegant, minimal, professional direction shows the same offer with calmer trust cues suited to that comparison.

### Broad / universal — friendly / clear / universal (szeroka grupa odbiorców)

Many local visitors are mixed-age and not fashion-led; a friendly, clear, universal direction keeps the candidate-attracting story readable for the widest neighborhood audience without leaning hard into youth energy or corporate polish.
